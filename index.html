TAPP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

TAPP is a tap-based game with global leaderboards. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and who else sees it.

The short version

TAPP has no signup. There is no email address, no password, and no real name attached to your account. What we hold is a player name, a country, and the scores you earned.

What we collect

You give us:

  • Username — a name generated for you when you first open the app (for example quickfox_21), which you can replace with one you choose. It is shown publicly on the leaderboards, which is the whole point of it.
  • Country — a two-letter country code. Taken from your device's language and region settings when your account is created, and changeable by you at any time. Shown publicly next to your scores.

Created automatically when you play:

  • An anonymous account ID — a random identifier that lets our servers tell players apart. It is not derived from your device, your Apple or Google account, or anything that identifies you personally.
  • Gameplay events — for each run, the timings of your taps and, in Dodge, where on the screen you tapped. We use these to calculate and verify your score on our servers, which is how we keep fake scores off the leaderboards.
  • Your scores, and when you set them.
  • Whether you have purchased ad removal.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or your location. TAPP never requests location access. Your country comes from a device setting and from your own choice — not from GPS, and not from your IP address.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles the anonymous accounts. Your username, country, scores and gameplay events are stored there.
  • Google AdMob — serves the banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and information about your use of the app. On iOS this only happens if you allow it when asked; if you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — processes the ad-removal purchase and records that you own it. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog — product analytics. Receives which screens you open and what you do in the app, tied to your anonymous account ID, so we can see what players use and what they get stuck on. Hosted in the EU. See https://posthog.com/privacy
  • Expo — delivers app updates. See https://expo.dev/privacy

Apple and Google handle the payment itself. We never see your card details.

If you buy ad removal, ads stop and AdMob stops receiving anything.

Keeping and deleting your data

Scores are kept indefinitely, because the all-time leaderboard is a permanent record.

You can delete your account from Settings. Because there is no email or password on it, an anonymous account cannot be recovered — deleting it removes your username, your ranks and your entire score history from every leaderboard for good. The app warns you before doing this.

This also means we usually cannot identify your account from an email you send us. If you want your data removed and you still have the app installed, tell us your exact username and country at myboroughuk@gmail.com and we will do our best to find and delete it.

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to complain to the ICO (https://ico.org.uk).

Children

TAPP is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. The app shows advertising and is rated accordingly.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.
