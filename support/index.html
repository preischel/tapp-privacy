TAPP Support

TAPP is a tap-speed game with global leaderboards. If something is wrong, email myboroughuk@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Before you write

  • Your account has no email or password. To find it, tell us your exact player name and country as they appear on the leaderboard.
  • Changing your name or country — Settings → PLAYER → EDIT.
  • Deleting your account — Settings → DELETE ACCOUNT. This removes your scores from every leaderboard and cannot be undone.
  • Bought ad removal on another device? Settings → RESTORE PURCHASE.
  • A score lower than the number you saw? Every run is verified on our servers, which count taps up to 20 per second. Faster than that is not a human hand, so those taps are not counted.
